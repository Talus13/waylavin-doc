# Northmen

hardy, yet kind people
scattering of tribes
value the weight of the heart, and the strength of your voice. the northmen are an odd bunch. still a scattering of tribes, but united well enough. you will never find a more polite people, despite their relative...loudness. the northmen came to be, from a few refugees, that fled north. where the refugees came from, no one seems to know. wherever they came from, i feel we would know, considering the size of these gigantic people. there is reason they have so many nicknames. there is a LOT to talk about them. 'giants of north' 'the kind wintermen' and 'the great horn-men' are but a few of the ones i heard growing up. Either way, you will not get a warmer welcome, then if you came home to your mother making a stew with the whole family! still, despite their kindness, i do not think you will find a more...violent party people. they tend to get drunk, and break whole houses, with their brawls. they do not lie, as they seem no point in playing with words. in their OWN words, 'your voice is meant to be raised, not muffled by yer petty greed! now sing a skal with us!'
they seem to, as i said, have many nicknames, but the two they seem to prefer are 'northmen' the common name, and 'skjulindar' in their tongue.
