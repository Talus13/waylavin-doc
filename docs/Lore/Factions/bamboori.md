# Bamboori

eastern people
specializing in innovation and strength of the mind, and peace through the body. the bamboori get their name from the strange, reed like hats they wear, that they call 'bamboo' they do not seem to mind being called 'bamboori' as it is entertaining to them, it seems. when asked what they would prefer to be called, they tend to be rather enigmatic, about the origin of their people. from what little that could be gathered from waylavin scholars, they were once a large collection of warring tribes, until a leader arose, claiming to have achieved a strength so great, through peace of mind. Trandiqui was his name, and he united the warring tribes, through puzzles, problems, and philosophy. he alone changed the way the bamboori learned, and let them grow as they could. 'shoot and rise to the sky, like the bamboo from which you are named, my friends. the only limit, is the one which you set in your mind'.
