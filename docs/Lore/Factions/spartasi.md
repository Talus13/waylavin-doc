# Spartasi

warriors who value the weight of steel, in both blade, and gold. the spartasi were originally split, two island nations that were focused on entirely different methods of combat, to try and win the war between them. eventually, they both realized they were actually being puppeted; the leaders of both factions were using each other to get rich. needless to say, this led to a rather large revolt. this taught the spartasi much, key amoung them, is that if you are going to be greedy, be aware of a blade. merchants and warriors are treated with near the same respect, in this warm climate. warriors for the ability to protect those around them, and merchants, for making sure the money never stays stagnant. trade is the lifeblood of the Spartasi people. without trade, they would all fall apart. it is due to this economic value, that rich people are sparse; a truly rich person, does not trade. therefore, they do not help. a well made blade is a sign of status, as it represents well spent coin, and practical use for the coin. while greedy, you will never find a people that love parties more. they had been known to bankrupt full towns, on some occasions....
